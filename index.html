Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know Arabic before I start?

No. Beginners are welcome, including students who are starting with the letters.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged through PayPal after your free trial. We confirm the details with you on WhatsApp.

How do I book a lesson?

Complete registration and the placement test first. Your teacher reviews the test and then contacts you on WhatsApp to coordinate the trial.

Can children join?

Yes. A parent or guardian should complete registration and help coordinate lessons for students under 18.
